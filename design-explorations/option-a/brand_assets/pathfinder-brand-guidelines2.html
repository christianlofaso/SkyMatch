01The brand in one breath

Honest by default.
Never hypey.

Pathfinder was built by someone who sent 400 applications and got three interviews. The whole product is an argument against the spray-and-pray approach, so the brand has to sound like it means that. Plain, specific, a little blunt. Talk to an anxious sophomore the way a sharp friend who already made it through would.

3

The number that started it

400 applications. Three interviews. A 0.75 percent hit rate. That story is the brand. Lead with real numbers and real mechanisms, not promises.

What the mark means

A single marker, centered and held inside a steady frame. The dark square is the focus Pathfinder imposes: a small, deliberate space instead of an endless feed. The vermilion diamond is the one role worth your attention, set dead center where you cannot miss it. It is the whole thesis, the shortlist and not the spray, compressed into one shape.

05Voice and tone

Say the true thing

Every line should sound like it could survive a skeptical sophomore reading it at 1am. Concrete over clever. Mechanism over magic. If a sentence could appear on any other job tool, rewrite it.

Say this

400 applications. 3 interviews. Mass applying is broken, so we built something that is not mass applying.

Not this

Revolutionize your job search with AI-powered magic and unlock unlimited opportunities!

Say this

This one is a reach. Here is exactly why it might still be worth a shot.

Not this

Land your DREAM job effortlessly. Your future starts now!!!

Say this

We found two people who can refer you in. Both went to UIUC. Here is how to reach out.

Not this

Supercharge your network and 10x your reach with our seamless, world-class platform.

—

The em dash rule

Hard rule. No em dashes. No en dashes used as dashes. Anywhere. Copy, UI, marketing, email.

Use a period when two thoughts can stand apart. Use a comma when they lean on each other. Rewrite when neither fits. Hyphens inside compound words stay fine: warm-intro, big-tech, real-time.

Words to never ship
revolutionarygame-changingsupercharge unlock10xseamlesseffortless magicdisruptsynergycutting-edge world-classrockstarninjanext-gen
a

Lead with the number

A real statistic beats an adjective every time. Show the hit rate, the count, the reasoning.

b

Name the mechanism

Say what the product actually does. Warm intros through shared background, not "smart matching."

c

Talk like a person

Say "you," not "users." Contractions welcome. Read it aloud. If it sounds like a brochure, cut it.

d

Admit the hard parts

A reach is a reach. Trust comes from being straight about odds, not from inflating them.
