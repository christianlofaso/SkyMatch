Pathfinder is not another place to fire off more applications. It is a way to send far fewer, to the roles that were worth it all along.

01How it works

Three steps. No spreadsheet.

The setup is paste and go. The work is in what happens after, where your profile gets read the way a sharp friend would read it.

STEP 01

Paste your profile

Drop a LinkedIn URL or your resume text. Add a few words on what you want if you like. That is the whole setup.

STEP 02

We read it like a person

Your coursework, projects, and skills, not just keywords. We weigh what you have actually done against what each role needs.

STEP 03

Get fifteen with reasons

A shortlist across four buckets. Every match tells you why it landed there, so you decide where the effort goes.
