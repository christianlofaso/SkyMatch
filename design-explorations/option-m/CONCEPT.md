# option-m — Smarter infinite feed

SkyMatch's three curated bands (Strong / Worth a look / Stretch) stay as the trusted top tier, but below "Stretch" a **"More for you"** feed streams ranked roles that never bottom out, and it gets sharper every time you act. Each feed row carries a **save** bookmark, a **"Not for me"** skip beside it, and a one-line italic "why" (*"Matches your Python + fintech-risk saves"*); the honest **"Tuned to your saves"** caption names exactly what's steering it. Saving builds a positive affinity vector (the company types + skills you bookmark), skipping builds a negative one — together they re-rank the feed *within* relevance, so a save visibly bumps look-alike roles up (with a brief ember pulse) and a skip slides the row out and pushes its near-neighbours down. It never inverts the spine: a weak role can't out-rank a Strong one no matter how you click, and a labeled **"Worth a stretch"** off-pattern role is woven in on a cadence so discovery stays alive. **Everything is clickable:** the row opens the full detail drawer (Location · Term · Company · Why you fit · Skill alignment · Apply / Save / Full analysis), save and skip persist in localStorage and reflect instantly in the sidebar count, scroll-load (IntersectionObserver) AND a manual "Load more" both append the next batch from a ~39-role pool, and a **"Picking up where you left off · 14 new roles added"** resume marker greets returning users — while a calm cold state (*"Save or skip a few — your feed sharpens fast"*) covers the first visit. The pull-back is the loop itself: the feed is endless, steerable, and demonstrably remembers you, so there's always one more relevant role and a reason to come back and teach it more.
