Why Warm Intros Work

Most online applications never reach a human. They get filtered by an applicant tracking system or buried under hundreds of near-identical resumes. That is the application black hole, and spraying more applications into it does not change the math.

A warm intro skips the queue. Candidates with an internal referral are far more likely to get an interview, because a hiring team trusts a name it already knows. The fastest way in is a person, not a portal.

Pathfinder is built on that idea: find the roles that genuinely fit you, then find the alumni and contacts who can vouch for you, so your application actually gets read.
