# option-n — Tracker + Alerts (one retention loop)

option-n fuses the two retention prototypes into a single coherent loop: **alerts are the intake (what's arriving), the pipeline is the destination (what you're moving through).** Fresh matches land in your **saved searches** → you triage them into your **application pipeline** → you manage the funnel to its deadlines. One merged welcome-back subtitle is the only freshness anchor (*"Welcome back, Maya — 7 new in your searches · 2 deadlines this week."*). The **pipeline strip** (Saved · Applied · Interviewing · Closed, live tabular counts, "X tracked · Y in flight") replaces the old stat cards and is always visible — clicking a tile filters the feed to that stage. The **saved-searches rail** (All + *SWE · Bay Area* / *Risk & Fintech* / *Near campus*, each with an ember-glow new-count pill, plus "+ Save this search") replaces the company-type chips and scopes the feed; the Best fit / Newest sort sits beside it. The honest readiness **bands** render below: a new, unseen role wears a **"New" dot + left ember rail** until opened, and a tracked role wears a **status tag** (`Applied · Jun 14`) — a role can show **both** at once without clutter. The **drawer is the merge point**: Why-you-fit + Skill alignment, plus the status stepper (Saved→Applied→Interviewing) + deadline field, plus a provenance line (*"Matched: Risk & Fintech"*). Opening a role clears its New dot; setting a status moves it into the pipeline, tags its row, and ticks the tiles. **Load more** is continuous intake — it appends roles and raises the active search's count. Everything (`sm:pipe`, `sm:saved`, `sm:seen`) persists to localStorage, so both the unseen alerts and the in-flight funnel survive every reload — giving two reasons to come back: see what's new, and advance what you've already started. Honest mechanics throughout: status is never auto-advanced, deadlines are user-entered (flagged only within 7 real days), counts clear only on a genuine open — no countdowns, no shame metrics.
