# option-l — Application tracker

SkyMatch stops being a one-time lookup by becoming the place students *manage the chase*, not just discover it. A collapsible **pipeline strip** docks at the top of Matches with four live count tiles — **Saved → Applied → Interviewing → Closed** (tabular-nums, ticking as you move roles). The three readiness bands render unchanged below. The retention loop lives in the **drawer**: alongside Why-you-fit and Skill-alignment, a status stepper (Saved → Applied → Interviewing, plus a quiet *Mark closed* / *Not tracking*) and an optional **deadline** field. Setting a status is always user-confirmed — never auto-marked — and it instantly moves the role into the pipeline *and* tags its row in the bands (`Applied · Jun 17`). **Clicking a tile filters the feed** to just that stage across all bands, so "what's interviewing?" is one tap. A calm **funnel ribbon** surfaces only honest, actionable counts — `2 deadlines this week · 1 reply to log` (deadlines are the student's own dates within 7 days; no manufactured countdowns, no shame metrics) — collapsing to *"You're on top of your pipeline"* when clear. A soft **Nudge** pill appears on roles saved but untouched past a week (*"Saved 9 days ago — still open?"*). A bottom **Load more** feeds fresh intake into the bands so the list never bottoms out, giving new candidates to push into the funnel. Statuses and deadlines persist to localStorage, so the pipeline — and the reason to come back and advance it — survives every reload.
