# option-k — New-match alerts

SkyMatch stops being a one-time lookup by becoming a set of **standing searches it watches for you**. A "Your searches" rail pins above the Strong band: each saved search (*SWE · Bay Area*, *Risk & Fintech*, *Near campus*, plus *All matches*) carries a live **new-count pill** that glows ember when fresh roles have landed since your last visit, and a persistent **"+ Save this search"** turns the current filters into another alert. A calm, dismissible **"7 new matches since Tuesday — 3 Strong"** ribbon is the freshness anchor; new roles thread into the *existing* readiness bands (never a separate "New" band) wearing a left ember rail + **"New" dot** until viewed. Everything is clickable: the drawer opens with full Why-you-fit / Skill-alignment, saves toggle and persist (localStorage), a chip filters the whole feed to that search and re-scopes the ribbon, **opening a role clears its dot** (counts recompute instantly, and an empty rail flips to "All caught up. We check every few hours."), and **"Load more"** pulls the next page of inventory for the active search — the list never bottoms out. The pull-back loop is honest: the only thing that ever increments a count is a genuinely new posting matching a search the student chose, and the only thing that clears it is the student actually looking — no fake urgency, no countdown, no inflation.
